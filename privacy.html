Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Arivora Ltd ("Arivora", "we", "us") builds practice-management software for New Zealand general practice. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we look after it. We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 and, where health information is involved, the Health Information Privacy Code 2020.

1. Who this policy covers

It covers two groups of people:

  • Visitors to arivora.co.nz, including people who book a demo or contact us.
  • Users of the Arivora application at app.arivora.co.nz, which practices use to run their non-clinical operations.

If you are a staff member or patient of a practice that uses Arivora, the practice is responsible for your information under the Privacy Act. Arivora holds that information on the practice's behalf. Questions about how a practice uses your information should go to the practice first. Section 6 explains more.

2. What we collect on the website

Our website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. We collect information only when you give it to us:

  • Demo and contact requests: your name, role, practice name, email address, phone number and anything you write in your message. The form is processed by a form-handling service and delivered to us by email.
  • Email you send us at hello@arivora.co.nz or support@arivora.co.nz.

We use this information to respond to you, arrange demos and trials, and keep in touch about Arivora. We do not sell it or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

3. What we collect in the Arivora application

Account information

When a practice invites you to Arivora we hold your name, work email address, role and practice, your password in encrypted form, optional two-factor settings, and records of when you sign in. We use this to give you access, keep your account secure and provide support.

Practice information

Practices enter their own records into Arivora: staff files and documents, onboarding details, rosters and leave, policies and sign-offs, compliance records, incident reports, meeting minutes, equipment and templates. Some of this is sensitive personal information, including tax numbers, visa and police-vetting details, and incident reports may include patient identifiers. The practice decides what to enter, and we process it only to provide the service to that practice.

Usage information

We keep a history of changes made in the application so that practices have an audit trail, and we record technical events such as errors so that we can keep the service running. We may use aggregated statistics about how features are used, which identify no person, patient or practice, to improve Arivora.

Problem reports

When a user sends a problem report from inside the application, we receive the description and technical details needed to investigate it.

4. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, secure, support and improve the Arivora service;
  • set up practices and user accounts and send invitation, account and service emails;
  • respond to enquiries, demo requests and support requests;
  • bill practices and manage their subscriptions;
  • meet our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use personal information for any purpose that is not connected to running Arivora, and we do not send practice information to any artificial-intelligence service outside Arivora. Ora, the assistant inside Arivora, answers only from the practice's own records and Arivora's built-in help.

5. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with:

  • the service providers we use to host the application and its data, send email and process website forms, each of which is bound by contract to protect it and to use it only to provide their service to us;
  • the practice you belong to, which can see the information held in its own account;
  • professional advisers where necessary, under confidentiality;
  • regulators, law enforcement or courts where the law requires it.

We never sell personal information.

6. Information held for practices

When a practice uses Arivora, the practice is the agency responsible for its records under the Privacy Act 2020. Arivora acts as the practice's agent under section 11 of that Act. This means:

  • the practice decides what information is entered and who in the practice can see it;
  • we access a practice's records only to provide support the practice has asked for, to investigate a security or technical problem, or where the law requires it;
  • Arivora staff cannot see patient details in a practice's records;
  • a practice can download all of its information from Arivora at any time;
  • when a practice leaves Arivora, we delete its information in accordance with our Terms of Service, other than copies in routine backups, which are overwritten in the ordinary course.

7. Where information is stored

Arivora's data is held on secure cloud servers in Australia. New Zealand privacy law continues to apply to it, because Arivora remains responsible for it and our providers are bound by contract to protect it. We will not move information to any country without comparable privacy protections.

8. How we protect information

  • Each practice's information is separated from every other practice's, and this is enforced in the database itself.
  • All connections to Arivora are encrypted.
  • Sensitive identifiers, including tax numbers and patient identifiers, are encrypted in the database, and access to sensitive fields is restricted by role.
  • Access is by invitation only, with email and password and optional two-factor codes.
  • Every change is recorded in a history that cannot be edited.
  • The service is backed up automatically every day.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a privacy breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the affected practice promptly, and we will notify the Privacy Commissioner where the law requires it.

9. How long we keep information

  • Website enquiries: as long as needed to respond and follow up, and no longer than 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Account and practice information: for as long as the practice uses Arivora, then deleted in accordance with our Terms of Service.
  • Billing records: 7 years, as New Zealand tax law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email us at hello@arivora.co.nz. We will respond within 20 working days. If your information is held in a practice's Arivora account, we will refer your request to that practice, which is responsible for responding, and we will help it do so.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change materially affects how we handle practice information, we will tell practices by email before it takes effect.
